# Copy bank

Everything here is ready to paste. Nothing is a template to be "adapted" — if a
post needs adapting it is in the wrong section.

**Placeholders.** Anything in `[square brackets]` must be replaced before
posting. Four recur:

| Placeholder | Where the real value is |
|---|---|
| `[dish]`, `[Wednesday]`, `[$18]` | Dashboard → This Week, for the week you are posting about |
| `[4:00–7:00 PM]` | Dashboard → Locations & Delivery — the pickup window. Seeded at 4–7; check it |
| `[10 PM]` | The cutoff. Seeded at 22:00. Dashboard → Settings |
| `[phone]` | Settings → owner contact. It is also printed on the back of the business card |

**A post with an unreplaced bracket in it is the campaign's one unforgivable
mistake.** Read every post out loud before it goes anywhere.

```
How ordering works, since a few people have asked.

1. Open dinnerbyderek.ca and look at the week. Every day, every dish, the
   prices, and what's already sold out.
2. Pick your day and what you want. Pickup or delivery — type your postal code
   and it'll tell you straight away whether I come to you and what it costs.

   is cooked the day you get it, so I need to know the night before.

   password, nothing to download.

You'll get an email with your order on it and a reference to put in the
transfer.
```

### A4 — The allergen review  · week 4 · Page and groups

The trust post. Plain, no adjectives.

```
Something the ordering site does that I want to explain, because it's the part
I'd want to know about if I were buying.

It will not let me publish a week's menu until every dish on it has been
through an allergen review. Not a warning I can click past — it refuses. Each
dish, every week, including ones that ran last week, because what matters is
this dish on this menu.

It reads the dish against a dictionary of 479 ingredients and terms and shows
me what it thinks is in there. I accept what's right, correct what isn't, and
tick it off. Until all of that is done, the menu doesn't go up.

It doesn't make my kitchen allergen-free and I'd never tell you it did. What it
means is that when you read "contains: milk, wheat" on this menu, somebody sat
down and checked, on purpose, before you saw it.

If you've got an allergy, tell me anyway when you order. But you shouldn't have
to take my word for the list.

dinnerbyderek.ca
```

*Image: `allergens.png`.*

> **Do not shorten this one.** The caveats are what make it credible. A trimmed
> version reads as a safety claim, which it is not and must never become. See
> [STRATEGY.md → Never say](STRATEGY.md#never-say).

## Replies and small print

The questions that will get asked, answered the way they should be answered.
Keep these consistent; inconsistency is what makes a small business feel
unreliable.

**"Do you deliver to [somewhere]?"**
> Type your postal code on the site and it'll tell you on the spot — quicker
> than waiting for me. The list is most of Kitchener and Waterloo. If you're
> outside it, pickup is [4:00–7:00 PM] and you're very welcome.

**"Is there a minimum order?"**
> [Check Dashboard → Locations & Delivery before answering. There is a delivery
> minimum setting and it may or may not be switched on. Answer with the number
> or with "no minimum" — never with "I think so".]

**"Can I pay cash?"**
> Yes — cash on collection, or e-transfer. There's no card payment and nothing
> goes through the website.

**"Do I need an account?"**
> No. No account, no password, nothing to download. It's a website — you can put
> it on your home screen if you use it often, but you don't have to.

**"Can I order for tonight?"**
> Not tonight, sorry — everything's cooked the day you get it, so orders close
> at [10 PM] the night before. If it's after that and before six in the morning
> the site will let you send a request instead, and I'll say yes or no.

**"What if I have an allergy?"**
> Every dish on the menu has been through an allergen check before it goes up,
> and what's in it is listed on the day. Tell me when you order as well — I cook
> everything in one kitchen, so I'd rather know.

**"Is this a subscription?"**
> No. Order the weeks you want, skip the rest, and nothing renews.

**"What happened to ordering in the comments?"**
> Nothing — comment away. The site's there if it's easier, that's all.

**Somebody is rude about the price.**
> Don't argue and don't justify at length. One line: "It's [$18] for [dish],
> made that day. Not for everyone, and that's fair enough." Then leave it.

**A moderator removes a group post.**
> "No problem at all — thanks for letting me know, I'll keep it out of the
> group." Nothing else. Do not ask them to reconsider.

## Things that are true and usable

A stock of specifics, for when a post needs one. Everything here was true on
2026-09-09; check anything that carries a number before printing it.

- Three years of weekly menus, and **813 saved dishes** in the library.
- **479** ingredients and terms in the allergen dictionary.
- **15** postal codes across Kitchener and Waterloo.
- Two soups and two salads most weeks, because the menu has offered a choice of
  two for three years.
- Soup by the litre. Dessert. A meatless main.
- Orders close at **[10 PM]**; nothing at all is taken after six the next
  morning.
- Collection **[4:00–7:00 PM]** — a window, not a booked slot. Come when it
  suits.
- The site is hosted in **Quebec**, so names, addresses and phone numbers stay
  in Canada. Worth one line, once, if the audience is the kind that cares. Not a
  headline.
- No app store, no account, no password, no card payment, no subscription.
