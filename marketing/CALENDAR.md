# Calendar

Six weeks. Weeks are numbered rather than dated, because the start depends on
when the four checks in [README → Before anything is posted](README.md#before-anything-is-posted)
are done, and because a plan with dates in it rots the first time a week is
skipped.

**Week 1 starts on the Saturday you build a menu week having finished those
checks.** Everything hangs off Saturday, because that is when the week gets
built and the menu post goes out.

---

Every week, campaign or no campaign. This is the rhythm the campaign sits
inside, not something the campaign adds.

## After week 6

The campaign ends. What survives it:

| Keeps running | Stops |
|---|---|
| The two lines on every menu post | The three Quiet Move posts — said once, never repeated |
| A card in every bag | The six acquisition posts |
| `last-call.png` the night before each service day | |
| One group post a month, rotating groups | Two posts a week |
| Flyers replaced when a board is cleared | |

One group post a month is a maintenance rate. It keeps the business visible to
people who have not met it without turning the groups into a channel you are
obliged to feed.

## Rules for moving things around

Written down so a rearrangement stays sane.

1. **The menu post is never moved to make room for a campaign post.** It is the
   business; the rest is marketing.
2. **Never two posts to the same audience on the same day.** The Saturday menu
   post and A1 are on the Page together in week 3, which is the one exception —
   they run together because A1 explains the post above it.
3. **Never the same text into two groups on the same day.** People are in both.
4. **A skipped week is skipped, not compressed.** Do not post three things the
   following week to catch up.
5. **If the kitchen is behind, the campaign stops that week.** Every post is a
   promise that somebody will cook.
6. **Nothing is scheduled.** Every item here is a paste, done by a person who
   has looked at the week first. An automated post is a post that goes out
   about a menu that is not up.
